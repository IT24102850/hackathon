Context & AI Methodology

Understanding the Challenge

Detailed technical breakdown of the disaster landscape in Sri Lanka, vulnerabilities in current mechanisms, and the mathematical architecture of our predictive system.

1. The Sri Lankan Problem

Tropical island geography, extreme precipitation events, and severe monsoon vulnerability

Sri Lanka is acutely susceptible to catastrophic hydro-meteorological disasters governed by a bimodal monsoon system—the Southwest Monsoon (May to September) and the Northeast Monsoon (December to February)—as well as intensifying inter-monsoonal tropical depressions. In November 2025, Cyclone Ditwah struck the island, generating historic rainfall that triggered unprecedented flash flooding and catastrophic hill-country debris flows, displacing over 2 million individuals and incurring upwards of $4.1 billion in nationwide infrastructure devastation.

Key Critical River Basins Most Prone to Overflow:

Kelani Ganga Basin Kalu Ganga Basin Gin Ganga Basin Nilwala Ganga Basin Mahaweli Ganga Basin Deduru Oya Basin

2. Who is Affected

Vulnerable populations, agriculture, and high-risk districts

Floods and landslides disproportionately affect over 2 million residents across both low-lying river floodplains and unstable escarpments. Agrarian rural communities face existential crises: over 58,000 hectares of paddy farming were inundated in recent cycles. Urban informal settlements situated on floodplains experience prolonged waterlogging, destruction of sanitation, and contamination of potable water, while children, the elderly, and hospital patients face acute displacement.

High-Risk Districts Directly Monitored:

Kalutara Ratnapura Galle Matara Colombo Gampaha Kegalle Badulla Nuwara Eliya

3. What Currently Fails

Shortcomings of conventional threshold-based warning

Conventional early warning infrastructure in Sri Lanka relies predominantly on static meteorological rainfall thresholds (e.g., 100mm in 24 hours). This rule-based paradigm exhibits fatal limitations:

  • Ignores Antecedent Soil Moisture: Soil saturated from rain weeks prior will liquefy or cause overland runoff at far lower rainfall levels than dry ground.
  • Lagging Administrative Notification: Warnings travel through bureaucratic hierarchies, frequently arriving hours after floodwaters peak.
  • Opaque Shelter Availability: Evacuees are directed to shelters without knowledge of capacity, causing bottlenecks at filled sites while nearby centres remain empty.

4. How Risk Score is Calculated

Multi-signal XGBoost predictive machine learning pipeline

The system predicts disaster probability using an optimized XGBoost classifier trained on 50 years (1974–2024) of verified DesInventar disaster records, evaluated via 5-fold TimeSeriesCV:

RiskScore = σ( Σ w_i · f_i ) ∈ [0.0, 1.0]
Features: [Rainfall_Now, Rain_Prev1, Rain_3Mo, Rain_6Mo, SRTM_Elevation, Terrain_Slope, Rain×Slope, Month_Sin, Month_Cos]

• Critical Risk (≥ 60%): Immediate proactive evacuation alert.
• Moderate Risk (35% – 59%): Heightened monitoring & prep.
• Low Risk (< 35%): Standard seasonal operations.

5. Prototype Limitations

Scientific bounds, operational constraints, and roadmap
  • Satellite Observation Latency: NASA GPM IMERG satellite products carry an inherent 3-to-4 hour processing lag for near-real-time early runs.
  • Spatial Aggregation: Predictions operate at the District level rather than micro-scale Grama Niladhari (GN) divisions, necessitating local judgment for specific valleys.
  • Absence of Live Hydro-telemetry: Current release does not ingest live telemetry sensors from Irrigation Department river stage gauges.
  • Sample Shelter Occupancy: Safe centre occupancy counts reflect simulated/sample field data until live integration with DMC camp management software is finalized.

Evacuation Checklist

  • Pack dry rations, non-perishable food, and potable water bottles.
  • Keep National Identity Cards, deeds, and prescriptions in waterproof zip bags.
  • Turn off domestic mains electricity switches and LP gas regulators.
  • Relocate livestock and pets to elevated, open enclosures.
  • Check the Safe Centres tab to confirm vacancy before departure.
